Scope

This policy covers ScrapYardVR by HYFAI Studio. It addresses game data on your device, Meta platform authorization, and information you choose to email to support.

Local game data

The game saves money, completed orders, unlocked devices, tool upgrade level, tutorial completion, discoveries, collectibles, and audio, handedness, comfort, movement and language preferences. The purpose is to retain progress and settings. The save is stored on the device. The in-game reset option deletes the local save file.

Meta platform

On Quest, the game initializes Meta Platform and checks whether you are entitled to use the app. It receives the check result to allow or block access. The checked game code does not request or save a Meta user ID. Meta may process account, device and authorization information under its own policies. Meta controls its own storage and deletion.

Other services and sharing

The checked game code has no game-operated account, player upload feature, game-operated server endpoint, or calls to advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. The Meta authorization service is the identified platform interaction. The presence of an SDK does not mean every feature of it is enabled.

Support email

If you contact HYFAI Studio, we receive your email address and the details you choose to send, such as device model, game version and reproduction steps. We use these to respond to your request. Email is handled through the relevant email providers.

Deletion and contact

Use the in-game reset option to remove the local game save. For Meta-held information, use Meta's account and privacy controls; HYFAI Studio cannot delete that information on Meta's behalf. For questions or requests concerning support messages, email 304797181@qq.com.
